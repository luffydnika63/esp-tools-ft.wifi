Twin Deauth
Clones the target BSSID on the same channel. Bypasses MFP on modern devices.
Packet Deauth
Sends raw 802.11 deauth frames. Supports multi-target. Devices with 802.11w may resist.
Handshake
Captures WPA/WPA2 4-way handshake. Outputs .pcap & .hccapx.
PMKID
Clientless — captures from first EAPOL-Key frame. Works on most WPA2 APs.
Beacon Spam
Floods airspace with fake beacons, polluting Wi-Fi scan lists on all nearby devices.
Evil Twin
Clones target AP and execute deauth. Fake Captive portal harvests the network password.
Ghost Mode (Probe Spam)
Exploits persistent device probing for saved networks. Clones those SSIDs so devices attempt to associate with the ESP32 instead of the original AP.
BT Deauth
BLE connection flood — targeted or all. Disconnects nearby BLE peripherals. Stable, use Stop to abort.
BT Spoof
Clones a selected BT device (name + MAC). Impersonates it in scans. Needs one BT target selected.
BT Crash
SourApple crash — malformed Apple BLE continuity packet that reboots/crashes nearby iOS (lab only).
BT Jammer
High-rate random BLE flood on 3 channels. Degrades 2.4 GHz BT/WiFi for nearby devices. Short bursts.
BT Sniffer
Passive 10s BLE scan + logger. Enumerates nearby devices for targeting Spoof/Deauth. Stable, auto-finishes.
